## 1. Decision statement (verbatim, as received)

> Decision Statement: 2D-ification Frontier Visualization — A Route of Exhaustive Constraint Enumeration and
> Distinguishability Mapping
>
> Date: 2026-11-22
> Status: Submitted for approval
> Nature: Standalone, self-contained text, superseding all previous revisions and incremental drafts
>
> **I. Objective**
>
> To establish the physical scene of the 2D-ification process as a 2D-ification frontier sweeps across a macroscopic
> 3D object. This project abandons the linear "diagonal traversal" model in favor of an exhaustive two-dimensional
> mapping: enumerating all possible combinations of physical constraints against four tiers of structural
> distinguishability. The route is defined as a coverage set rather than a sequential path, ensuring that every
> constraint combination is rigorously evaluated to determine the maximum achievable distinguishability under strict
> physical laws.
>
> **II. Constraint Set**
>
> | No. | Constraint | Current Status |
> |---|---|---|
> | C1 | Effective 2D-ification space (column conservation d_parallel approx 2.91 × 10^{-4} a_0) | Established (identity-level) |
> | C2 | Smooth 2D-ification (zero intra-layer strain) | Holds within layers; inter-layer interface failure registered (8.12 × 10^9 interfaces) |
> | C3 | Weak interaction preservation (self-consistency within the action) | In-action NO-GO established (rho + p_x < 0, parameter-independent) |
>
> **III. Distinguishability Tiers (Columns of the Matrix)**
>
> The vertical axis of the research matrix defines the operational criteria for structural recognition in the
> 2D-ified scene:
>
> - **L0 (Topological Distinguishability)**: The 2D scene preserves the topological invariants (e.g., genus,
>   connectivity) of the original 3D macroscopic object. This is the absolute minimum threshold for physical
>   self-consistency.
> - **L1 (Metric Distinguishability)**: The 2D scene is "close" to the original 3D structure under a specific,
>   optimized metric space (e.g., Hausdorff or Wasserstein distance). The specific metric and its threshold are
>   selected individually for each constraint combination to best fit its physical characteristics.
> - **L2 (Information-theoretic Distinguishability)**: The mutual information I(Scene; Structure) between the 2D
>   scene and the 3D object exceeds a threshold I_{min}. This threshold is dynamic and declared independently for
>   each constraint combination.
> - **L3 (Visual Distinguishability)**: The 2D scene is recognizable as a structured image by a naked-eye observer
>   without the aid of digital enhancement (no false color, band migration, or contrast stretching). This represents
>   the ultimate goal of "real physical scenery."
>
> **IV. The Research Matrix (Route Structure)**
>
> The project is executed as a data matrix where rows represent constraint combinations and columns represent
> distinguishability tiers.
>
> **Phase 1: Exhaustive Enumeration (C1–C3)**
>
> The system must traverse all 2^3 = 8 possible combinations of the base constraints (C1, C2, C3). For each
> combination, the maximum achievable distinguishability tier (L0–L3) is determined and recorded.
>
> | Constraint Combination | Max Distinguishability | Notes |
> |---|---|---|
> | C1 ∧ C2 ∧ C3 (Full) | TBD | Strict physical baseline |
> | C1 ∧ C2 ∧ ¬C3 | TBD | |
> | C1 ∧ ¬C2 ∧ C3 | TBD | |
> | ¬C1 ∧ C2 ∧ C3 | TBD | |
> | C1 ∧ ¬C2 ∧ ¬C3 | TBD | |
> | ¬C1 ∧ ¬C2 ∧ ¬C3 | TBD | |
> | ¬C1 ∧ ¬C2 ∧ C3 | TBD | |
> | ¬C1 ∧ ¬C2 ∧ ¬C3 (Zero) | TBD | Minimal constraint baseline |
>
> **Phase 2: Constraint Extension**
>
> If and only if all 8 combinations in Phase 1 fail to reach L3 (Naked-Eye Visual), the project enters the extension
> phase. New constraints (C4, C5, …) are introduced to modify the physical landscape.
>
> - Nature of New Constraints: Unlimited in number and type (geometric, optical, material, etc.). They may interact
>   with C1–C3.
> - Selection Mechanism: Not pre-defined. Determined dynamically by the research process to maximize structural
>   distinguishability.
> - Termination: The process continues until a constraint set yields an L3 result.
>
> **V. Execution Protocol**
>
> - Strict Physicality: All scenes generated for the matrix must be strictly physical. Digital enhancement is
>   permitted only as a tool to verify L1 or L2 during the audit process, but L3 requires naked-eye visibility of the
>   raw physical output. Enhancement means must never introduce information that does not exist physically.
> - Metric & Threshold Declaration: For any cell achieving L1 or L2, the specific metric used and the I_{min}
>   threshold declared must be explicitly registered in the matrix cell.
> - Negative Verification: The project must explicitly prove that combinations failing to reach L3 indeed fail. A
>   "null image" or "structureless noise" result is a valid matrix entry.
> - No Sequential Bias: The order of evaluation for the 8 base combinations is irrelevant; the requirement is
>   complete coverage.
>
> **VI. Bottom Line and Discipline**
>
> - No Falsified Imagery: No cell in the matrix may present content that contradicts established NO-GO conclusions
>   (e.g., intact lamellae persisting in ordinary-matter states).
> - Auditability: Every entry in the matrix must be traceable to a specific physical derivation or simulation run.
> - Threshold Integrity: Thresholds for L1 and L2 must be declared before the evaluation of the scene to prevent
>   post-hoc fitting.
>
> **VII. Immediate Action Items**
>
> 1. Baseline Generation: Commence the generation of strictly physical scenes for the C1 ∧ C2 ∧ C3 (Full Constraint)
>    case to populate the first row of the matrix.
> 2. Metric Definition: Establish the library of candidate metrics for L1 evaluation and the methodology for
>    calculating Mutual Information for L2 evaluation.
> 3. Governance: Register this "Exhaustive Constraint Enumeration" route as the governing decision statement,
>    superseding the "Phased Constraint Relaxation" route.
>
> One-sentence version: The project is a systematic mapping of the constraint-distinguishability landscape,
> exhaustively evaluating all C1–C3 combinations against four rigorous tiers of structural recognition—from
> topological invariance to naked-eye visibility—and extending the constraint space only when the base set proves
> insufficient to yield a physically real, visually structured 2D world.
