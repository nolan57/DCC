[G-3] THE ME5 COROLLARY CARRIES AN UNSTATED PREMISE: D = L
==============================================================================
  Frozen identity (correct, and not attacked):
      t_rip = sqrt(2) L / c     2L/c = sqrt(2) t_rip
  Frozen inference (attacked):
      therefore no reflected-light frame of a surviving sheet can exist, for any L.

  The identity uses L twice: as the object size (destruction path) and as the optical
  path.  The optical path for a camera at distance D is 2D; it equals 2L only when
  D = L.  D is an observation geometry, not a physical constant, and no registered
  text states it.

  Per-patch form.  A patch at distance d from the tear origin converts at
  t = d/v_front with v_front = L/t_rip = c/sqrt(2) (the Phase 2 definition, not a new
  front speed).  It can be photographed iff the round trip fits in its own lifetime:

        2D/c <= d/v_front      <=>      D <= d/sqrt(2)

  The admissible set is the annulus d >= sqrt(2) D -- strictly beyond a half-angle
  atan(sqrt(2)) = 54.7356 deg off the camera axis.  It is non-empty iff D < L/sqrt(2).
  Covered fraction of the radial extent = 1 - sqrt(2) D/L.

    D/L       admissible?   covered fraction   half-angle to far edge
    ------------------------------------------------------------------
    0.0500    yes                +0.929289    87.1376 deg
    0.2500    yes                +0.646447    75.9638 deg
    0.5000    yes                +0.292893    63.4349 deg
    0.7000    yes                +0.010051    55.0080 deg
    0.7071    NO                 +0.000000    54.7356 deg
    1.0000    NO                 -0.414214    45.0000 deg

  The registered corollary is the D = L row: 1 - sqrt(2) = -0.414214, i.e. NEGATIVE.
  A negative admissible extent is not a physical impossibility; it is the signature
  of an observation geometry outside the window.  Reading it as "impossible for any
  L" replaces an out-of-range parameter choice with a result.

  Per channel at L = 0.20 m, v_front = 2.119853e+08 m/s:
    D = 0.0100 m -> coverage  +0.9293   admissible sub-field
    D = 0.0500 m -> coverage  +0.6464   admissible sub-field
    D = 0.1000 m -> coverage  +0.2929   admissible sub-field
    D = 0.1414 m -> coverage  +0.0002   admissible sub-field
    D = 0.2000 m -> coverage  -0.4142   EMPTY (the registered slice)

  Verdict: the IDENTITY stands; the INFERENCE does not follow without D = L.
  ME5 is frozen, so the correction is registered as GD8 and NOT applied.

  Time deficit for the reflection row uses the registered D = L slice; the flux

    channel                      time     flux
    ------------------------------------------
    transmission 284 eV        0.0000   1.9795
    transmission visible       0.0000   9.9795
    reflection visible         0.1505   9.9901

  Pareto relation:
    transmission 284 eV      strictly dominates  transmission visible
    transmission 284 eV      strictly dominates  reflection visible
    transmission visible     strictly dominates  reflection visible

  READING A — fidelity = how much the frame shows.
    transmission 284 eV dominates reflection-visible on BOTH coordinates, so FL2 is
    the Pareto maximum and FL3 is strictly dominated.  Order A: FL2 > FL3 — the
    ladder DESCENDS toward its declared end point.
    Robustness: the flux coordinate is independent of D, and transmission-visible also
    dominates reflection-visible there, so this order survives the GD8 correction.

  READING B — fidelity = how close the frame is to the unaided retinal image.
    conditions, in order: band inside 1.8-3.1 eV | photon budget met unaided | no enhancement operator | displayed quantity is a light field
      FL1  ....   unmet 4/4
      FL2  .Y.Y   unmet 2/4
      FL3  YYYY   unmet 0/4
    Order B: FL3 > FL2 > FL1 — the ladder ASCENDS toward its declared end point.

  THE TWO READINGS REVERSE THE LADDER.  Reading A is what every quantitative
  criterion here measures (CNR, photon counts, exposure budget).  Reading B is what
  the goal sentence says ("to naked-eye visible reflection").  "Walk the entire
  diagonal" presumes a total order that no registered quantity supplies.
  Registered as GD7: declare the semantics of FL, or the ladder has no direction.
