Conclusion: the three assumptions conflict pairwise; no parameter point satisfies all of them at once.
  K1 eff-2D  <-> composition: L_z window [1.68,1.99] fm, registered value outside
  K2 eff-2D  <-> known physics: requires 4.7e+05 x E_S
  K3 z wall pressure <-> lateral budget: still off by a factor 25 under the most permissive convention
  K4 energy ledger <-> 2.74%: misses the confinement zero-point energy by a factor 13 (lower bound)
  K5 smooth  <-> timescale separation: none (ratio 0.63)
  K6 weak-on <-> stable final state: logically mutually exclusive
Note: none of the above depends on the values of X_p / mu_e / P, so it is orthogonal to the B2 convention dispute.
