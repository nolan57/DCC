[M-8] sIII vs sVI -- the same threshold, two incompatible rules
==============================================================================================
  sIII L1 : 'The specific metric and its threshold are selected individually for each
             constraint combination to best fit its physical characteristics.'
  sIII L2 : 'This threshold is dynamic and declared independently for each constraint
             combination.'
  sVI     : 'Thresholds for L1 and L2 must be declared before the evaluation of the
             scene to prevent post-hoc fitting.'

  These cannot both hold.  A threshold chosen per cell, to best fit that cell, IS
  post-hoc fitting; that is what the phrase forbids.  And from M-3 the fitting has a
  price: at C1 the fitting metric must carry 3.79e+11 bits of pi.

  Adjudication (executor's, for the governor): keep sVI, delete both 'selected
  individually' / 'dynamic' clauses.  Reason: sVI is the invariant that makes a matrix
  cell mean anything, and sV independently forbids the information the fitting injects.
  Consequence, stated so it is not a surprise: with sVI alone, the fixed metric must be
  chosen once, globally -- and then M-3 says which way it scores.
