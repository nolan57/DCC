[7] WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY -- derived from the tables above, not from the registration
==================================================================================================
  1. RELIEF IS NOT WHAT MAKES CONTRAST. The deposit is a near-mirror (|dR| = 1e-3), and under a
     uniform environment a mirror is invisible: every reflected ray carries the same radiance, so
     relief changes the path and not the pixel. C_w equals C_flat EXACTLY (difference 0e+00)
     for both forms, over six decades -- not "to within noise", exactly.
     "Topographic contrast" is a property of the ENVIRONMENT, which
     theta = (aperture, gate, band, distance) does not carry. That is the missing term, and it is
     the one the experimenter sets when the source is brought -- at 284 eV there is no sun.

  2. In the collimated channel ("full sun", beta = 4.65e-3) the number is reachable, but as a
     narrow ramp: the sine reaches C_w = 0.01 at 68.4 um and 90% of its peak by 368.0 um, a factor
     5.38 in amplitude -- and the terraced form is an outright switch at 1.18.
     The crossing a* = 68.8 um sits inside that ramp, and it is the geometric condition
     2*alpha_max = beta. Below it the plate is a mirror-reflected sun; above it, a fringe pattern.

  3. The price variable does not enter. Terraced relief switches at 26.6 um (factor 1.18) and then
     holds C_w = 0.280 for a further 3.6 decades: 0.280 at 100 um, 0.280 at 1 mm. Contrast for a
     topographic EDGE is set by the riser width and by the environment, not by the step height.
     RL-2 is priced in a/pitch. The measurement says a/pitch is not the variable that buys anything.

  4. So the 3e-2 is DECLARED, and it is the criterion restated as a measurement: Q3 = 2 needs
     C = 3*C_eye = 0.030000 and the ladder wrote 3e-2. Round 48's "zero margin" is not the
     measurement of a cliff in physics; it is what one significant figure looks like when it is
     copied from the criterion it is supposed to test. Measured, the rung sits at 0.28 for a 1 mm
     step and at 3.32 for a 1 mm corrugation, and at neither of those is it near 3e-2.

  5. eps_max = 0.3182 is real and it is the deposit's own number, but the useful part of the rung is
     [0, 1.7e-04]: 99.95% of its declared V range buys nothing at all.

  6. What is left of the rung after this: relief is a real knob and it is nearly free (eps ~ 1e-4),
     but its price cannot be quoted as a number until theta carries an environment term and the
     relief form is declared. Both are declarations, not measurements -- which is why this round
     closes the question "is 3e-2 measured" with NO, and leaves "what is the rung worth" to a
     declaration the ladder has not made.
