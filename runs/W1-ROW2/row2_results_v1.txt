      Counting the calf->thigh entries as per-piece areas gives V = 0.06111 m^3,
      i.e. 1.68% above the registered 0.0601 m^3. N9' is not edited; this cycle records the
      reconciliation. Consequence: the volume fraction *can* be printed for this mechanism,
      but this cycle still declines to print a continuous profile because the table gives
      per-region ranges, not a profile. The refusal stands; its stated reason does not.

      59.8% of the deposited volume has already left the 2D state. Row 2 therefore cannot
      present a completed deposit: the 'after the sweep' frame is mostly decayed matter, and
      only the last 3 ns of swept material is still converted. This is a property of the
      declared transient, not a derivation of it (tau is imported).

  F3  The declared optical probe can neither excite nor resolve the z degree of freedom at
      0.215 nm, yet the cell is still NOT-MEASURED: 'cannot see z' and 'is 2D' are different
      claims, and the missing slot is the confining potential -- not the probe.

  F4  The profile does not rank the two fields, and the tiers move in opposite directions:
      the canvas field is smoother than the sheet field (xi = 39.9 vs 19.0 cells) yet has
      lower legibility (Q3 = 4.49 vs 7.56 at the same q = 1). A single 'structure score' would
      have to choose between them. Every cell also PASSes its threshold, so the tiers separate
      these scenes from the null ensemble (Q0 ~ 0, Q2 ~ 0, xi < 1) but do not discriminate
      between structured scenes. That is a property of the tiers, not of the scene.
