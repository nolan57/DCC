[Mutual exclusivity] why you cannot have both
  To be 'bright' requires energy at the 60 MeV scale (plasma)
  To have 'content' requires energy at the 5 eV scale (chemical bonds/interband transitions)
  The two differ by a factor of 1e+07 -- the same matter cannot be at both energy scales at once.
  [ARGUMENT] S_mutual_exclusion           PASS  brightness and structure come from energy scales differing by 1e+07, physically mutually exclusive
