# DCC — terminal answer v1 (D8)

**Round**: 62 · **Item**: RQ-11 · **Authority**: none — this is a reading of the gate table, the ratified governor marks and the terminal queue states. Where it disagrees with a ratified decision, the decision wins.

DCC is a decision-complete research programme asking whether a macroscopic 3D object can be reduced to an effectively 2D state at fixed projected area under physically consistent constraints, and what a declared observer could then receive; under `PG-03` it is a priced ladder rather than a single experiment, with the selected path `RL-1 + RL-2`, rung price `V = ε` (with `docs/specs/relaxation_ladder_v1.md` E2's qualification that `ε` is a dial whose measured useful travel is `5e−4` of its range, not a measured price), corrected flux line `Φ = −4.5` orders at the measured narrow-environment contrast `C = 3.3166` (`relaxation_ladder_v1.md` E3; the collimated terraced case gives `C = 0.280`, and both require the named environment `E` and camera `G` after `theta_environment_v1.md` / `theta_camera_v1.md`), and fingerprint "a 0.1 s exposure is empty where a sub-3 ns gate shows a frame, and the reflected environment is distorted by the known silhouette" (`relaxation_ladder_v1.md` §2); its reverse-on is `relaxation_frame_v1.md` §7 (no rung reaches `Q3 ≥ 2.0` at any `θ`, or total price exceeds a declared governor budget), with the `PG-03` Path-A window still open because no rung has been bought (`relaxation_frame_v1.md` §10). DCC is **not** a visibility, transparency, opacity or mirror statement (`S9`; `g6_dc2_ruling_v1.md` §6/§7), not a demonstration that ordinary matter survives fixed-area conversion (`G0′` row 1), not a readable-image result (`DEPOSIT-BLIND`; `route_terminus_v1.md` §9), not matrix-v2 governance (a product, not a route), not a pass of `G2′`, `G4` or `G5′`, not a relaxation or trade of `C3`, and not a claim that any gate beyond `G0′` and `G1` has fully passed: `G6` is `NO-GO` in the gate table and is superseded only at the decision level by `PG-01`'s `CONDITIONAL-ADMISSIBLE` mark, which binds four refinements, grades nothing downstream and carries the three §5 reversers. Every closed line below reopens only through its own named clause — a measurement, a governor instruction or a specification ruling — never through "more work", a rescued line or a re-priced rung.
