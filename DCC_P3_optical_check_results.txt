Verdict summary:
  1. The contraction ratio already reached in P2, C/C0~0.10 (transverse ratio C/A~1e-2), is insufficient for any macroscopic L0
     to become optically indistinguishable (the required ratio lambda_obs/L0 is far below 0.10).
  2. Under power-law extrapolation (C∝t^(beta_C), beta_C=-1/2), the required t/t0 = (lambda/L0)^(-2):
     L0=1m → t/t0~4e15 (infeasible); L0=1um → t/t0~4e3 (attainable in the asymptotic regime).
     That is: only when the initial "third-dimension thickness" is already micron-scale can the specified contraction background, in finite time,
     yield an optically indistinguishable third dimension.
  3. Conclusions (allowed/not allowed statements, corresponding to review checkpoint 2):
     Allowed: "Under the specified contraction background, the physical length in the Z direction contracts as a power law; for micron-scale initial thickness,
            optical indistinguishability can be reached in the asymptotic regime."
     Not allowed: "a macroscopic three-dimensional region has been compressed to optical invisibility" (the depth already reached in P2 differs by more than 7 orders of magnitude).
  4. If strict D3 (C→0) is achieved, optical indistinguishability for any L0 is attained at finite t —
     this is the physical meaning of the D3 criterion (constraint inherited from P2 report §10).
