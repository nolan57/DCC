  E4 is carried because it is the model behind every "topographic contrast" statement; the
  deposit's near-mirror excludes it, and round 49 already found the 1/c^2 lever only there.
  form  env  C 0 deg  C 45 deg  C 75 deg  C(75)/C(45)  tan75/tan45
  ----  ---  -------  --------  --------  -----------  -----------
  S     E4   0.0002   0.0201    0.0751    3.732        3.732      
  T     E4   0.0008   0.0217    0.0807    3.724        3.732      

  In E4 oblique illumination IS a lever, and it is a STRONGER one than the ladder claims:
  C(75)/C(45) = 3.732 against tan75/tan45 = 3.732, i.e. the response is tan(theta_i) to three
  digits. And at normal incidence the channel is nearly BLIND -- C(0) = 0.0002, four hundred times
  below the 75 deg value -- because the cosine law is STATIONARY in slope at theta_i = 0, so
  relief enters only at second order. That is the real reason a "topographic edge at 3e-2"
  needs obliquity, and it is a property of the diffuse model, not of the relief.
  E4 is the one channel that separates RL-2 from RL-3, and it is the channel this surface is not.

[9] WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY -- derived from the tables above, not from the registration
==================================================================================================
  1. RL-3 IS NOT A CONTRAST RUNG IN THE CHANNEL THE MATERIAL FORCES. For a specular surface the
     deflection angle is 2*arctan(alpha) and carries no incidence, so moving the source moves the
     camera's job, not the image: with the camera in the specular position C is IDENTICAL (0.0e+00
     difference in float64) across 0/45/75 deg, both forms, all five amplitudes. With the camera
     fixed the window leaves the surface at 2*psi_max - beta: 3.31 deg for the sine, 8.88 deg for
     the terrace, beyond which there is no image at all. Neither reading is a gain.

  2. FRESNEL BUYS PHOTONS, AND AT 75 DEG IT LOSES THEM. R(75)/R(0) <= 1+4/Z0*sigma_s: at most 5%
     for a near-mirror, and 0.03% at the record's own eV value. The projected solid angle falls by
     cos(75) = 0.259, so the net is -0.61 orders at Z0*sigma_s = 80 and -0.59 at 1e4. For a 2-D
     sheet the loss is structural: R_p -> 0 at grazing while R_s -> 1, so the unpolarised average
     DROPS. Oblique illumination on this object is not free; it is a bill.

  3. OCCLUSION IS THE ONLY MECHANISM THAT MOVES C, AND IT IS GEOMETRY. A facet shadows when
     tan(theta_i) exceeds its slope, so at a = 1 mm the discrete riser (slope 0.080) never shadows:
     the threshold is a > 3.35 mm at 75 deg. Half the tread shadowed costs eps = 0.0335, i.e. 195 x
     the eps at which the glint channel is already at threshold. It is also independent of L_e: a
     shadow is dark under any narrow environment. That makes it RL-2's geometry seen from a
     different angle, not a second relaxation.

  4. THE LADDER'S FINGERPRINT IS INVERTED, AND ITS AXES ARE CONFLATED. "Contrast collapses to the
     RL-0 value under normal incidence" is false in the narrow-width column: C(0 deg) = 3.3166, i.e.
     Q3 = 8.38. What collapses is a WIDE environment (E1), at every incidence. The rung conflated
     beta with theta_i, and got the sign of the theta_i dependence wrong for a specular surface:
     contrast is MAXIMAL at normal incidence and degrades towards grazing, not the reverse.

  5. SO RL-3 IS RETIRED AS A RUNG, NOT RE-PRICED. Its price was already V = 0; this round measures
     B = 0 as well -- it delivers no bits the other rungs do not. What remains of it is a
     DECLARATION (which environment the experimenter brings: docs/specs/theta_environment_v1.md)
     and a HAZARD (a fixed camera at 45 deg on this material sees a mirror-reflected void).
     Neither is a purchase, and the ladder should say so rather than list a third free rung.

  6. WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED. In E4 oblique illumination IS a lever -- C tracks tan(theta_i), a factor
     3.73 between 45 and 75 deg and 431 between 0 and 75 deg -- but E4 is the diffuse model the
     near-mirror surface excludes, and round 49 already found the 1/c^2 lever only there. The
     separation of RL-2 from RL-3 therefore exists exactly on the model the object is not.
